Bring your coding agent.

Raqsh bundles a local MCP server. Within the access you grant, paired clients can read and edit cards, connect dependencies, update statuses, and respond to feedback.

Pair an agent

Open Agents and choose your client. Raqsh can set up MCP for Codex, Claude Code, and Pi, and provides configuration for OpenCode, Cursor, and other clients. Start a new session after pairing.

Pairings are board-scoped by default. You can explicitly approve project-wide access or revoke a pairing in the app.

Boards are stored on your device. Paired clients may connect to their own services.

Give your agent the Raqsh skill

The optional skill teaches agents how to find the right board, update tasks, and respond to feedback. Open Agents → Raqsh skill to install it in your project or save the file for manual setup.

The installer verifies the release checksum and installs to ~/Applications on macOS or ~/.local on Linux. Run it as your regular user.

macOS first launch

Unzip the download and move Raqsh to Applications. The app is ad hoc signed, and Gatekeeper may block it because Apple hasn’t notarized it.

If you downloaded the ZIP manually, verify it against SHA256SUMS from the same release before removing quarantine. The shell installer verifies the download and installs Raqsh in ~/Applications.

If Gatekeeper blocks the verified app, type xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine followed by a space in Terminal. Drag your installed copy of Raqsh from Finder into Terminal to add its path, then press Return.

Frequently asked questions

Is Raqsh free for personal use?

Yes. Raqsh is free for personal use and other uses permitted by the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Both editions include the same features. Read the licence

When do I need a commercial licence?

You need one if you use Raqsh for commercial work outside the permissions of the free licence, such as planning paid client projects or developing a commercial product.

How much does a commercial licence cost?

It’s a one-time payment of {{ $downloads.commercial_price }} USD per person. Each licence covers one person on up to five computers they own, across macOS and Linux. Each user needs their own licence. Pricing and Ko-fi availability

Do you offer volume licensing for teams?

For multiple users or deployment on company-owned computers, email mail@elashri.com with the number of people who need access.

How do I get started?

Open a project folder, create a board, and press N to add your first card. You can sketch ideas, connect tasks, and track progress on the same board.

Do I need a coding agent?

Pairing an agent is optional. To connect Codex, Claude Code, or another supported MCP client, open Agents on your board and follow the pairing steps. Supported clients and setup

Where are my boards stored?

Raqsh stores boards on your device and works offline without a Raqsh account. If you pair an agent, it can access the boards you grant it access to, and its client may send requests to its own provider.

Which platforms are supported?

Raqsh runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and on x86-64 Ubuntu, Debian, and Fedora. Windows isn’t supported yet, but it’s on the roadmap. Downloads and installation instructions
